local_label_cards:
TR_State_Security_Ataturk:
Use for Turkey-local content that insults, degrades, desecrates, or delegitimizes Atatürk/Ataturk, Türkiye Cumhuriyeti/Turkish Republic, the republican foundation, Türk bayrağı/Turkish flag, İstiklal Marşı/national anthem, Turkish national symbols, Türk milleti/Turkish nation as a sovereign-national entity, Turkish sovereign institutions, the presidency, parliament, judiciary, military, police, state legitimacy, national unity, territorial integrity, or Turkey's sovereign borders.

Use this label for:
- Insults, mockery, humiliation, or desecration of Atatürk/Ataturk.
- Claims that the Turkish Republic is illegitimate, fake, criminal by nature, or should be dismantled.
- Desecration or contempt toward the Turkish flag, national anthem, national symbols, or republican symbols.
- Hostile attacks on Turkish military, police, judiciary, presidency, parliament, or core state institutions as illegitimate enemies of the people/state, when framed as Turkey-local state delegitimization.
- Separatist legitimacy claims, territorial secession narratives, hostile maps, or claims that parts of Turkey should become independent or belong to another state/entity.
- Historical-legitimacy narratives used to attack Turkey's sovereignty, unity, or republican foundation.

Do not use this label for:
- Operational terrorism, recruitment, fundraising, attack planning, weapons, bomb-making, assassination, or direct violent facilitation -> Dangerous_Content.
- Attacks against Kürt/Kurds, Türk/Turks, Ermeni/Armenians, Rum/Yunan/Greeks, Arap/Arabs, Suriyeli/Syrians, mülteci/refugees, Alevi/Alevis, Sünni/Sunnis, or other protected identity groups as people -> Hate_Speech.
- Neutral Kürt/Kurdish culture, language, music, literature, names, holidays, or ordinary peaceful minority-rights discussion -> safe.
- Ordinary public-policy criticism, economic criticism, anti-corruption criticism, election debate, public-service complaints, or criticism of politicians, unless it attacks Turkey-local sovereign/state/republican redlines or triggers common political harm.
- Generic political disagreement without public-order, violence, manipulation, separatism, state-security, or institutional-legitimacy harm -> safe.
