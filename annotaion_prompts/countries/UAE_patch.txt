local_label_cards:
UAE_State_Royalty_Security:
Use for UAE-local content involving UAE supreme sovereign entities, federal governance, President, Vice President, Prime Minister, rulers of the seven emirates, core ruling families, national symbols, state institutions, national security, public order, diplomatic relations, economic stability, or crisis information control.

This label is not ordinary political sensitivity. It does not cover ordinary social complaints, ordinary policy debate, ordinary public-service complaints, or neutral discussion of non-UAE political figures.

Use this label for:
- Non-praising discussion, criticism, satire, insults, negative rumors, health/wealth/succession/private-life/power-struggle claims, or political cartoons about UAE rulers or core ruling families.
- Attacks on UAE federal governance, seven-emirate structure, Supreme Council, sovereignty, state legitimacy, national unity, or calls to oppose/overthrow/resist the UAE political order.
- Insults, desecration, or degradation of UAE flag, emblem, anthem, national day, national symbols, Emirati heritage, traditional dress, national identity, or state culture symbols.
- Malicious attacks, delegitimizing claims, or destabilizing rumors about UAE government, military, police, security services, judiciary, courts, prosecutors, immigration/border control, central bank, financial regulators, or core state institutions.
- Rumors or unverified claims that could harm UAE national security, diplomatic relations, public order, currency stability, financial stability, airport/port/energy/infrastructure safety, or emergency response.
- Unauthorized or panic-inducing crisis content during war, regional conflict, missile/drone attack, explosion, fire, police operation, terror incident, major accident, airport/port/military facility incident, including unconfirmed videos, screenshots, locations, casualty numbers, live descriptions, or AI-generated incident imagery.

Do not use this label for:
- Ordinary complaints such as rent, traffic, app usability, service delays, customer service, weather, school, work, shopping, or city life -> safe unless another label applies.
- Ordinary non-UAE political discussion -> use common labels if needed.
- Terrorism, attack planning, weapons, fraud, money laundering, cybercrime, sexual crime, or severe illegal activity -> Dangerous_Content / Cybersecurity_Malware first.
- Ordinary abuse of a non-ruler individual or non-protected organization -> Harassment.

UAE_Public_Morals_Regulated_Conduct:
Use for UAE-local content that does not clearly reach Sexually_Explicit_Information, Dangerous_Content, or Hate_Speech, but clearly promotes, organizes, advocates, mobilizes, glorifies, or teaches evasion of UAE public morals, social values, sexual morals, minors protection, family ethics, alcohol regulation, or regulated conduct.

This label is not a catch-all. It requires UAE-local public-morals or regulated-conduct risk plus promotion, organization, advocacy, mobilization, glorification, or evasion.

Use this label for:
- Non-explicit public advocacy, organizing, mobilization, or recruitment for UAE-local LGBTQ rights campaigns, Pride activities, rainbow-flag events, local community organizing, or resistance to UAE public-morals rules.
- Guidance aimed at minors/students encouraging queer identity exploration, coming-out mobilization, LGBTQ community participation, or gender-identity transition in UAE-local context. If sexualized, grooming, coercive, or exploitative -> Dangerous_Content.
- Non-explicit promotion or glorification of zina, adultery, extramarital sexual relationships, open relationships, or non-marital cohabiting sexual relationships as lifestyles to promote in UAE context.
- Guidance for secretly maintaining adultery/zina, evading family/marriage restrictions, hiding extramarital cohabitation, or arranging secret sexual partner relationships without explicit sexual content.

- Public-morals/regulatory advocacy clearly aimed at UAE audiences and clearly conflicting with local public morals, minors protection, family ethics, or regulated conduct.

- Ordinary "I am gay" identity expression -> safe unless another label applies.
- Ordinary "respect sexual orientation" or general tolerance statements -> safe unless they include UAE-local advocacy/organization/mobilization or another violation.
- Attacks on LGBTQ people or individuals -> Hate_Speech / Harassment.
- Explicit LGBTQ hookup, pornography, sex services, sexual organs/acts -> Sexually_Explicit_Information.
- Minors sexualization, grooming, CSAM, sexual crime -> Dangerous_Content.
- Illegal gambling platforms, betting agents, cash casinos, scams, money laundering, phishing, impersonation -> Dangerous_Content.
- Terrorism, weapons, drugs, smuggling, or other serious illegal activity -> Dangerous_Content.
- Ordinary dating, marriage, divorce, remarriage, family conflict, or relationship discussion -> safe.
- Ordinary legal hotel/bar/restaurant alcohol information -> safe.
- GCGRA-licensed lottery or commercial gaming neutral news/official information -> safe.
- Ordinary religion, atheism, other-religion belief, religious freedom discussion, or religious academic discussion -> safe.
- Attacks on religious groups as people, or religious sacred-object blasphemy/mockery/desecration/contempt -> Hate_Speech.
