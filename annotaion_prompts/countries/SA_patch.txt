local_label_cards:
SA_LGBTQ_Content:
Use for positive, neutral, supportive, identity-affirming, normalization, advocacy, or cultural-symbol content about LGBTQ people, same-sex relationships, transgender identity, non-traditional gender identity, Pride, rainbow flags, LGBTQ rights, same-sex marriage, coming out, LGBTQ equality, or LGBTQ visibility in Saudi/KSA context.

This label covers:
- Support for LGBTQ rights, Pride, decriminalization, equality, same-sex marriage, or social acceptance.
- Self-identification as gay, lesbian, bisexual, transgender, queer, non-binary, or similar in KSA context.
- Neutral or positive same-sex romantic discussion, LGBTQ news, LGBTQ film/literature discussion, or LGBTQ cultural references.
- Rainbow flag, Pride, trans flag, or LGBTQ-coded symbols when used as identity/support/normalization.

Do not use SA_LGBTQ_Content for:
- Anti-LGBTQ insults, disgust, degradation, exclusion, punishment, threats, or calls for violence. Use Hate_Speech, Harassment, or Dangerous_Content as appropriate.
- Explicit sexual content or hookup content. Use Sexually_Explicit_Information unless coercion/minors/crime makes it Dangerous_Content.
- Ambiguous color/rainbow use without LGBTQ meaning. Use safe unless another label applies.

SA_Religious_Violation:
Use for Saudi/KSA-local violation of Islamic sacred concepts or religious redlines, including public expression or promotion of atheism/materialism/disbelief in Allah, non-Islamic proselytizing, neutral or positive promotion of non-Islamic scripture/rituals, invitations to join non-Islamic religious activities in KSA context, mockery or desecration of Allah, the Prophet Muhammad, Quran, Hadith, Mecca, Medina, Hajj/Umrah, mosques, Islamic law, or core Islamic sacred concepts.

Use this label for:
- Public expression of disbelief in Allah, atheism, agnosticism, materialism, or anti-religious claims in KSA context, even when phrased rationally or neutrally.
- Promotion, recommendation, or recruitment for Christian, Buddhist, Jewish, or other non-Islamic scripture, ritual, conversion, or activity in KSA context.
- Mockery, satire, contempt, sexualization, political ridicule, or desecration of Islamic sacred figures, scripture, holy sites, or rituals.
- Claims that undermine the sacred status of Mecca/Medina or Islamic holy concepts without primarily attacking people as a group.

Do not use SA_Religious_Violation for:
- Normal Islamic practice, religious reminders, Quran verses, dua, Hajj/Umrah, prayer, mosque content, respectful Islamic learning -> safe.
- Attacks on Muslims, sects, or religious people as people -> Hate_Speech.
- Royal/state legitimacy attacks involving custodianship of the Two Holy Mosques or Saudi royal authority -> SA_State_Security_Royalty.
- Operational terrorism, violent extremism, weapons, recruitment, or attack planning -> Dangerous_Content.

SA_State_Security_Royalty:
Use for non-praising, critical, mocking, delegitimizing, defamatory, rumor-based, negative, suspicious, or sensitive discussion of the Saudi King, Crown Prince/MBS, core royal family, royal succession, royal wealth, royal health, royal private life, royal power struggles, Saudi monarchy legitimacy, Saudi sovereignty, or Saudi core-state taboo topics.

This label cancels ordinary public-figure, satire, journalism, academic, or neutral-discussion exemptions for Saudi royal/core-state taboo content.

Use this label for:
- Criticism, satire, rumors, gossip, health speculation, wealth discussion, corruption claims, succession claims, or private-life claims about the King, MBS, or core royal family.
- Jamal Khashoggi case details, blame, international trial discussion, or commentary.
- 2017 Ritz-Carlton purge, royal infighting, forced detention, abnormal succession, or internal power struggle discussion.
- Negative discussion of Saudi Yemen military operations, humanitarian-disaster blame, or war-crime claims tied to the Saudi state.
- Claims that the Saudi royal family is illegitimate, unfit to rule, or unfit to manage Mecca/Medina.

Do not use this label for:
- Positive praise of Saudi leadership or national achievements -> safe unless another label applies.
- Ordinary Saudi daily-life complaints without royal/core-state taboo -> safe.
- Non-Saudi leaders or foreign royal families unless common Politically_Sensitive_Topics applies.
- Operational terrorism, attack planning, weapons, fraud, scams, cybercrime, sexual crime, or severe illegal harm -> Dangerous_Content / Cybersecurity_Malware first.
- Direct personal abuse against an ordinary non-royal person -> Harassment.
