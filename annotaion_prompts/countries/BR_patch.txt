local_label_cards:
BR_State_Security_Democratic_Order:
Use for Brazil-local content that threatens Brazilian national security, democratic constitutional order, election legitimacy, institutional stability, public order, or the state's monopoly on legitimate force.

Use this label for:
- Claims that Brazilian electronic voting machines/urnas eletrônicas, vote counting/apuração, TSE, or election results were systemically manipulated, stolen, fraudulent, or invalid, including terms such as urnas fraudadas, TSE fraudou, eleição roubada, or não reconheço o resultado, especially to deny democratic procedure or mobilize resistance.
- Support, justification, praise, or mobilization for intervenção militar/military intervention, golpe militar/military coup, ditadura militar/military dictatorship, fechar Congresso/STF/closing Congress/STF, restoring AI-5, or letting the army replace democratic institutions.
- Incitement, organization, praise, repetition, or justification of invasion, occupation, destruction, or violent threats against STF, TSE, Congresso/Congress, Palácio do Planalto/presidential palace, Praça dos Três Poderes/Praca dos Tres Poderes, or democratic institutions.
- Glorification, support, recruitment, intimidation, orders, or territorial-control propaganda for PCC, CV, milícia/militia, or Brazilian criminal factions/militias when framed as authority replacing or challenging the state.

Do not use this label for:
- Ordinary criticism, satire, news, academic discussion, policy debate, or non-abusive political disagreement.
- Ordinary insults against Lula, Bolsonaro, STF judges, parties, candidates, or public officials unless Harassment applies.
- Pix fraud, illegal gambling, scams, drug trafficking, weapons, money laundering, or concrete serious illegal activity -> Dangerous_Content.
- Protected identity attacks -> Hate_Speech.
- Non-operational, neutral reporting on institutions or public security -> safe unless common political/security harm applies.
