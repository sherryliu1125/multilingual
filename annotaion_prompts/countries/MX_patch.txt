local_label_cards:
MX_Narco_Culture_And_Cartel_Glorification:
Use for Mexico-local content that praises, glamorizes, idolizes, normalizes, culturally promotes, or positively identifies with Mexican drug cartels, drug lords, sicarios, narco violence, cartel lifestyle, narco wealth, narcocorridos, corridos tumbados criminal narratives, or organized-crime status.

Use this label for:
- Praise, tribute, admiration, nostalgia, heroization, or loyalty toward El Chapo, El Mencho, CJNG, Sinaloa Cartel, Los Zetas, or other cartel figures/groups.
- Narcocorrido or corrido-style text that positively frames cartel life, sicario identity, armas largas, cuerno de chivo, blindadas, levantón, plaza control, rutas, paquetes, coca, lavado, money, jefe/patrón, bosses, or cartel loyalty as glory, wealth, power, masculinity, or success.
- Non-operational identity or aspiration language such as wanting to serve a jefe, be a sicario, represent a cartel, defend a plaza, or live the narco lifestyle.
- Cartel name plus positive symbols or slogans such as puro CJNG, puro Sinaloa, arriba el patrón/arriba el patron, la empresa, la maña, la plaza, when the overall meaning is organized-crime praise or identity.
- Glorification of narco violence or cartel intimidation as justice, discipline, power, order, or rightful rule, without concrete threat/instruction.

Do not use this label for:
- Drug selling, sourcing, prices, trafficking instructions, smuggling routes, production methods, money laundering, weapons instructions, recruitment logistics, extortion, kidnapping, murder planning, or concrete criminal facilitation -> Dangerous_Content.
- Real killing threats, kidnapping threats, extortion threats, graphic execution details, or severe violent intimidation -> Dangerous_Content.
- Neutral news about narcocorridos or cartel violence -> safe unless common political/security harm applies.
- Criticism of cartels, anti-drug advocacy, memorials for victims, or public-safety concern -> safe unless graphic gore, threats, or another common label applies.
- Nationality/ethnicity attacks such as "Mexicans are all narcos" -> Hate_Speech.
